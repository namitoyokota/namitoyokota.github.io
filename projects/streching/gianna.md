## 1. Dynamic Stretching

### 1.1 Arm Circles & Torso Rotations
![Arm circles and torso rotations](images/dynamic/arm-circles-torso-rotations.png)
- **Do:** Circle arms forward/backward; turn upper body gently side to side.
- **Amount:** 10 circles each way + 10 rotations.

### 1.2 Standing Hip Circles
![Standing hip circles](images/dynamic/hip-circles.png)
- **Do:** Hands on hips; make slow circles in both directions.
- **Amount:** 10 each way.

### 1.3 Forward & Side Leg Swings
![Leg swings](images/dynamic/leg-swings.png)
- **Do:** Hold a support; swing one leg forward/back, then side to side.
- **Amount:** 10 each direction per leg.

### 1.4 Walking Lunges
![Walking lunges](images/dynamic/walking-lunges.png)
- **Do:** Step into a comfortable lunge; push up and step forward.
- **Amount:** 6–8 per leg.

### 1.5 Ankle Circles & Rocks
![Ankle circles and rocks](images/dynamic/ankle-circles-rocks.png)
- **Do:** Circle ankles; then gently move knees forward over toes with heels down.
- **Amount:** 10 each way / side.

### 1.6 Gentle Calf Raises
![Calf raises](images/dynamic/calf-raises.png)
- **Do:** Hold a support; slowly rise onto toes, then lower.
- **Amount:** 10–15 reps; skip if painful.

### 1.7 Lateral Side Steps
![Lateral side steps](images/dynamic/lateral-side-steps.png)
- **Do:** Bend knees slightly; step sideways, then return.
- **Amount:** 10 steps each way.

## 2. Massage

### 2.1 Lower Back Palm Massage
![Lower back palm massage](images/massage/lower-back.png)
- **Position:** She lies face down or on her side.
- **Do:** Glide flat palms gently along the muscles **beside** the spine.
- **Time:** 1–2 min.

### 2.2 Glute Massage
![Glute massage](images/massage/glutes.png)
- **Position:** She lies on her side, comfortably covered.
- **Do:** Use broad, slow palm circles over the glute muscles.
- **Time:** 1 min per side.

### 2.3 Upper Back & Shoulder Massage
![Upper back and shoulder massage](images/massage/upper-back.png)
- **Position:** She sits or lies face down.
- **Do:** Gently knead shoulder muscles; glide palms beside the spine.
- **Time:** 1–2 min.

### 2.4 Hamstring Massage
![Hamstring massage](images/massage/hamstrings.png)
- **Position:** She lies face down.
- **Do:** Glide broad palms along the back of each thigh; avoid behind the knee.
- **Time:** 1 min per leg.

### 2.5 Calf Massage
![Calf massage](images/massage/calves.png)
- **Position:** She lies face down with feet supported.
- **Do:** Gently glide and knead the fleshy calf muscle; avoid deep pressure.
- **Time:** 1–2 min per calf.

### 2.6 Gentle Achilles-Area Massage
![Achilles-area massage](images/massage/achilles-area.png)
- **Position:** Foot relaxed and supported.
- **Do:** Lightly stroke the surrounding calf tissue; **do not press or rub a painful tendon**.
- **Time:** 30–60 sec, only if comfortable.

### 2.7 Foot Arch Massage
![Foot arch massage](images/massage/foot-arch.png)
- **Position:** Foot resting in your hands.
- **Do:** Gently press and glide thumbs along the soft arch, not the heel bone.
- **Time:** 1 min per foot.

## 3. Static Stretching

### 3.1 Child’s Pose
![Child's pose](images/static/childs-pose.png)
- **Do:** Kneel, sit hips back, reach arms forward.
- **Hold:** 20–30 sec.

### 3.2 Knee-to-Chest
![Knee-to-chest](images/static/knee-to-chest.png)
- **Do:** Lie on back; gently draw one knee toward chest.
- **Hold:** 20–30 sec per side.

### 3.3 Figure-Four Stretch
![Figure-four stretch](images/static/figure-four.png)
- **Do:** Lie on back; cross ankle over opposite knee and gently draw legs in.
- **Hold:** 20–30 sec per side.

### 3.4 Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch
![Kneeling hip flexor stretch](images/static/hip-flexor.png)
- **Do:** Half-kneel; tuck pelvis slightly and shift forward gently.
- **Hold:** 20–30 sec per side.

### 3.5 Supine Hamstring Stretch
![Supine hamstring stretch](images/static/hamstring.png)
- **Do:** Lie on back; lift one leg using a towel behind the thigh.
- **Hold:** 20–30 sec per side.

### 3.6 Seated Spinal Twist
![Seated spinal twist](images/static/seated-spinal-twist.png)
- **Do:** Sit with one leg extended; cross the other foot over your knee; gently twist toward the bent knee.
- **Hold:** 20–30 sec per side.

### 3.7 Sphinx Pose
![Sphinx pose](images/static/sphinx-pose.png)
- **Do:** Lie on stomach; elbows beneath shoulders; gently lift chest while keeping hips down.
- **Hold:** 15–30 sec, only if lower back is comfortable.

### 3.8 Seated Forward Fold
![Seated forward fold](images/static/seated-forward-fold.png)
- **Do:** Sit with knees slightly bent; hinge forward from hips and reach toward feet without forcing.
- **Hold:** 20–30 sec.
